1.  **Understand User Request:**
   - **Topic:** Compare optimistic concurrency control (OCC) vs. pessimistic locking (PL) for a collaborative document service.
   - **Requirements:** 
     - State the trade-off
     - Identify a failure mode for each
     - Give a practical hybrid design
     - Be precise and concise.

2.  **Deconstruct Key Concepts:**
   - *Optimistic Concurrency Control (OCC):* Assumes conflicts are rare. Reads without locking, checks for conflicts at write time (e.g., version numbers, timestamps). If conflict, abort/retry.
   - *Pessimistic Locking (PL):* Assumes conflicts are common. Locks resources during read/write to prevent concurrent access. Blocks other transactions until lock is released.
   - *Context:* Collaborative document service (multiple users editing the same document simultaneously).

3.  **Identify Trade-off:**
   - OCC: High throughput, low latency under low contention, but suffers from wasted work and retries under high contention.
   - PL: Guarantees consistency, prevents wasted work, but causes blocking, reduced concurrency, and potential deadlocks/starvation under high contention.
   - *Trade-off:* Concurrency/throughput vs. consistency/blocking. OCC favors performance when conflicts are rare; PL favors predictability and avoids wasted work when conflicts are frequent.
